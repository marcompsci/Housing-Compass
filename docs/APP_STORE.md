## Description
Housing Compass helps you find an affordable place to live and keep it.

FIND HOMES YOU CAN TRUST
• By default you only see homes from official housing portals or confirmed by the property manager.
• Unverified leads stay hidden unless you choose to see them, and they carry clear warnings.
• Room prices and whole-apartment prices are never mixed.
• Search by street, neighborhood, city or ZIP, draw an area on the map, and sort by total monthly cost.

AVOID RENTAL SCAMS
• Paste a message to check it for common scam signs.
• Never asked to pay before a tour: the app reminds you, every time.

PAY RENT ON TIME AND BUILD YOUR RECORD
• Log each rent payment and see your on-time streak.
• Get reminders 3 days before rent is due and on the due date.
• Export your payment history as a spreadsheet for a future landlord or a rent-reporting service.

KEEP EVERY BILL ON TRACK
• Add your phone, utilities, insurance and other bills next to rent and check them off each month.
• See what's left of your take-home pay after rent and bills.
• Check a rent-increase notice against California's statewide cap.

PROTECT YOUR DEPOSIT
• Make a dated, room-by-room move-in condition record with photos.
• After you move out, count down the 21 days California gives a landlord to return your deposit, and create a request letter.

STAY ON TOP OF EVERYTHING
• My plan shows rent, tours, application deadlines and waitlist check-ins in one list.
• Track program waitlists so you never miss a check-in.
• Find your city's official rent board and free tenant help.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your rent payments, bills, photos, notes, income and waitlists stay on your phone, with an optional Face ID lock.
• Your location is used once, approximately, and never stored on our servers.
• No ads, no tracking.

Available in English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. Housing Compass gives general information, not legal or financial advice.
