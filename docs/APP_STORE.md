## Description
Housing Compass helps you find an affordable place to live and keep it.

FIND HOMES YOU CAN TRUST
• By default you only see homes from official housing portals or confirmed by the property manager.
• Unverified leads stay hidden unless you choose to see them, and they carry clear warnings.
• Room prices and whole-apartment prices are never mixed.
• Search by street, neighborhood, city or ZIP, draw an area on the map, and sort by total monthly cost.

AVOID RENTAL SCAMS
• Paste a message to check it for common scam signs.
• Never asked to pay before a tour: the app reminds you, every time.

PAY RENT ON TIME AND BUILD YOUR RECORD
• Log each rent payment and see your on-time streak.
• Get reminders 3 days before rent is due and on the due date.
• Export your payment history as a spreadsheet for a future landlord or a rent-reporting service.

KEEP EVERY BILL ON TRACK
• Add your phone, utilities, insurance and other bills next to rent and check them off each month.
• See what's left of your take-home pay after rent and bills.
• Check a rent-increase notice against California's statewide cap.

PLAN EVERY PAYCHECK
• See which paycheck covers rent and each bill, and how much to set aside on payday.
• Forecast your balance for the next 30 days and get warned before it dips below $0.
• Import your bank's CSV to mark rent and bills paid — no bank login.

KNOW IF A HOME FITS
• Every listing shows how it fits your take-home pay and bills, and how long saving the move-in cost takes.
• See paydays and every due date on a money calendar.

GET OUT OF DEBT AND BUILD CREDIT
• Compare avalanche and snowball payoff plans.
• Check your credit card use and follow a simple credit checklist.
• Find money you may be owed: unclaimed property, tax credits and bill discounts from official programs.
• See what an extra payment each month saves in time and interest.

ASK YOUR MONEY ASSISTANT
• Ask in plain words — "Can I afford $40?", "What's due this week?", "When is payday?" — in English, Spanish or Chinese. Answers come from your own data on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
• Ask about rent, side income, taxes, subscriptions, trials, your emergency fund or any bill by name — or just "How am I doing?"
• See this month's money in and out, record part of a bill paid, log one-off money like a tax refund, and check your split against the 50/30/20 guide.
• When money is short, see what to pay first — housing and utilities, then minimums — and where to get help.
• Get a morning summary on days something is due.
• Track card and loan minimums with due days, split each paycheck, and get a heads-up before your lease ends.
• Track free trials and returns so you never pay for something you meant to cancel.
• Monthly money report and badges for real progress.

KNOW WHAT'S SAFE TO SPEND
• See what's safe to spend today after everything due before payday, and what's due this week.
• Ask "Can I afford this?" before you buy — yes, tight, wait until payday, or not now.
• Set a weekly spending limit, log spends in a tap and keep an under-limit streak.
• Split a tax refund or bonus wisely with the windfall planner.
• Track your net worth month by month, and take the 52-week savings challenge.
• See spending by category compared with last month, set monthly category budgets, and round up spends into savings.
• Hide amounts with one tap when you're in public.
• See each bill in hours of work.

EARN MORE AND KEEP MORE
• Track gig and side income, log business miles at the IRS rate, and set aside money for taxes — with the quarterly due dates.
• Plan for yearly costs like car registration and renters insurance with a monthly amount.
• Check a rent increase against San Francisco, Oakland, Berkeley and Los Angeles rent-control limits and the 2026–27 statewide cap.
• See whether your city requires interest on your security deposit.
• Track side-work expenses and your real pay per hour, and set a monthly side-income goal.
• Log calls asking for lower bills and see what they save over a year.
• Keep a who-owes-whom list with roommates and write a "your share" message.
• Get a "my year in money" summary from your own records.

TRACK YOUR CREDIT
• Know when each free weekly credit report can be checked again (AnnualCreditReport.com).
• Track hard inquiries and disputes, with the bureau's response date.
• Pay before your card's statement closes, and clear a promo rate before it ends.
• Keep a journal of the scores you see in your bank or card app (the app never estimates a score).
• Make a credit report dispute letter or a late-fee waiver request.
• See each card's use and how much to pay to get under 30% or 10%.
• Ask your landlord to report on-time rent, or ask for a payment plan, with ready-made letters.

PROTECT YOUR DEPOSIT
• Make a dated, room-by-room move-in condition record with photos.
• After you move out, count down the 21 days California gives a landlord to return your deposit, and create a request letter.

STAY ON TOP OF EVERYTHING
• My plan shows rent, tours, application deadlines and waitlist check-ins in one list.
• Track program waitlists so you never miss a check-in.
• Find your city's official rent board and free tenant help.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your rent payments, bills, photos, notes, income and waitlists stay on your phone, with an optional Face ID lock.
• Your location is used once, approximately, and never stored on our servers.
• No ads, no tracking.

Available in English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. Housing Compass gives general information, not legal or financial advice.
